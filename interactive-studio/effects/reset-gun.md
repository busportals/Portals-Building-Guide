# Reset Gun

## Use Cases

***

* Resetting a gun's ammo and position at the start of a new round
* Returning a dropped gun to its original spawn location
* Cleaning up gun state after a player is eliminated

## Configuration

***

No additional configuration needed. When triggered, the gun is reset to its initial state and position.
