# Exit Vehicle

## Use Cases

***

* Ejecting a player from a vehicle when they reach a destination
* Forcing players out of vehicles when entering a restricted zone
* Allowing players to exit a vehicle via an interaction

## Configuration

***

No additional configuration needed. When triggered, the player exits the vehicle they are currently in.
