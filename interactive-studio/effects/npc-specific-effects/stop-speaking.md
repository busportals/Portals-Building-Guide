# Stop Speaking

## Use Cases

***

* Ending the NPC's speaking animation after a dialogue line finishes
* Stopping the speaking animation when the player walks away
* Resetting the NPC to idle after a conversation ends

## Configuration

***

No additional configuration needed. When triggered, the NPC stops its speaking animation.
