# Change Enemy Health

## Use Cases

***

* Dealing damage to an enemy from a custom weapon or trap
* Healing an enemy during a scripted phase transition
* Setting an enemy's health to a specific value at game start

## Configuration

***

| Setting       | Permitted Values                   | Description                                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Operation     | Add, Subtract                      | Whether to add or subtract from the enemy's health.      |
| Health Change | Integer                            | The amount of HP to add or subtract.                     |
