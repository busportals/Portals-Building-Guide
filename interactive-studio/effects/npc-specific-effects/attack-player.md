# Attack Player

## Use Cases

***

* Triggering an enemy to attack when a player enters a zone
* Forcing a boss to aggro during a scripted encounter
* Making enemies react to player actions like stealing items

## Configuration

***

No additional configuration needed. When triggered, the enemy NPC attacks the nearest player.
