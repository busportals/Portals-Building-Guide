# Reset Enemy

## Use Cases

***

* Resetting enemies to their starting positions between rounds
* Restoring an enemy to full health and original location after a game reset
* Cleaning up enemy state when restarting a challenge

## Configuration

***

No additional configuration needed. When triggered, the enemy NPC returns to its original position with full health.
