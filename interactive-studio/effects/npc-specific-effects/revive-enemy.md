# Revive Enemy

## Use Cases

***

* Bringing back defeated enemies for a new wave of combat
* Reviving a boss after a phase transition
* Respawning enemies after a timer or event

## Configuration

***

No additional configuration needed. When triggered, the enemy NPC is revived.
