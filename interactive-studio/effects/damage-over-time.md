# Damage Over Time

## Use Cases

***

* Creating hazard zones that slowly drain player health (lava, poison, acid)
* Building environmental traps that punish players for staying in an area
* Designing damage fields around dangerous objects

## Configuration

***

This effect applies continuous damage to the player while they remain in contact with the trigger zone. No additional configuration is needed beyond the base interaction setup.

{% hint style="info" %}
This effect is best used with trigger zones. The damage applies for as long as the player remains inside the zone.
{% endhint %}
