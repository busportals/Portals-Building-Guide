# Stop Spectating Player

## Use Cases

***

* Returning a player to their own camera after spectating
* Ending spectator mode when a new round begins
* Allowing players to exit observation mode on demand

## Configuration

***

This effect has no additional configuration. When triggered, it immediately ends spectator mode and returns the player to their own camera view.
