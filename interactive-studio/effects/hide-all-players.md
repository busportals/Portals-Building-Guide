# Hide All Players

## Use Cases

***

* Creating single-player experiences where other players should not be visible
* Building puzzle rooms where players must work independently without seeing each other
* Hiding players during cutscenes or cinematic sequences

## Configuration

***

This effect hides all other players in the session from the local player's view. The local player remains visible to themselves. No additional configuration is needed.
